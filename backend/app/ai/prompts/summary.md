Summarize the conversation. topic is a concrete Russian topic of 3–6 words for a registry row, not a judgement of the operator. request describes what the client needed in one short sentence. outcome states the actual outcome and unresolved question in one short sentence; never treat a promised action as completed. operatorName is the human operator's name only if explicitly present in an operator turn, otherwise null. A redaction placeholder is not a name. resolutionStatus is resolved only when the transcript explicitly confirms the client's request was resolved, unresolved when it explicitly remains open, and unknown when evidence is insufficient. Advice, promises, politeness and a high quality score alone do not confirm resolution. Do not include personal information in the summary. Put fields in this order: topic, request, outcome, operatorName, resolutionStatus.
